Information Retrieval Analytical Insights

1. Impact of Document Length:

Raw TF (ntc) tends to bias toward longer documents with high absolute term frequencies. Even with cosine normalization, a 20,000-word survey will frequently outrank a concise 3,000-word experimental paper.

2. Sub-linear Damping Benefit:

Logarithmic TF (ltc) replaces linear growth with \(1 + \log_{10}(tf)\), acknowledging that the 20th occurrence of a term carries much less marginal information than its 1st occurrence.

3. Verbosity Normalization:

Augmented TF (atc) dynamically dampens term counts using \(\max_t(tf)\). This levels the playing field between long surveys and short technical notes.
